What ACCORD did

    V1 scope: single biweekly period, federal income tax only (no provincial income tax yet), no YTD-aware annual maximums, no retro adjustments, no supplementary benefits or garnishments. Real pay stubs may also include adjustments (e.g. LWOP, bilingual bonus, leave-with-income-averaging) that change the pensionable / insurable / taxable bases. If your stub differs by more than a few cents on any line, that's a finding worth raising with your pay centre — or telling us about. Provincial tax modules (starting with Ontario) and YTD-aware calculations are on the roadmap.
