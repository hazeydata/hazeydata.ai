The Person Behind the Data

Fred Hazelton
Data Scientist & ML Engineer
✉️ fred@hazeydata.ai 𝕏 @disneystatswhiz 💬 Discord

Fred Hazelton is an independent data scientist and former statistician at TouringPlans.com — where he spent years building the crowd prediction and wait time models that millions of Disney visitors rely on.

Now he's building a portfolio of data products under the HazeyData brand — from theme park crowd forecasting to sovereign AI infrastructure to national-scale data collection. Each project applies machine learning and data engineering to solve a specific, underserved problem.
