Features

What You Get

Everything you need to outsmart the crowds — powered by machine learning and delivered to Discord.

📊

Daily Wait Time Forecasts

Every park, every day. Our Wait Time Index (WTI) gives you one number that captures the whole park's wait time level.

🎢

Ride-by-Ride Predictions

See predicted wait times for individual attractions. Know which headliners to hit first and which rides will be walk-ons.

📅

Best Day Finder

Pick a park and a timeframe — we'll show you the lowest-crowd days with color-coded calendar heat maps, up to a year out.

🤖

Ask Anything

AI-powered Q&A backed by real data. Ask "Is July 4th a good day for EPCOT?" and get an instant, data-driven answer.

⏱️

Live Wait Times

See what's happening right now — estimated actual waits grouped by tier, updated every 5 minutes from real queue data.

💬

All In Discord

Seven slash commands, zero setup. /today /now /crowd /best-day /ask — everything lives where you already hang out.

The Science

How It Works

1

Years of Data

We ingest millions of historical wait time observations from every park, every day.

2

Machine Learning

XGBoost models trained per-attraction learn seasonal patterns, day-of-week effects, and park-specific trends.

3

Daily Forecasts

Fresh predictions every morning. Updated with yesterday's actuals. Delivered to your Discord.
