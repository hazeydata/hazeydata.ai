"The complexity of the Government of Canada HR and pay environment includes the challenge of applying almost 150 different collective agreements representing employees from over 100 departments and agencies." — Government of Canada, HR & Pay Transformation Announcement, June 2025

Following the Phoenix pay crisis, the Government committed to replacing its legacy pay infrastructure with Dayforce — a modern, cloud-based SaaS platform. The transition is underway, with first-wave departments expected to onboard by Winter 2027.

03
Current Remediation Paths

The GC's Options for Closing These Gaps

Policy & Regulatory Amendments

Change the rules to fit the software. Requires amendments to legislation, directives, and Treasury Board policies.

Long timeline

Collective Agreement Renegotiation

Negotiate new CA provisions with bargaining agents to simplify pay rules across ~150 agreements.

Multi-year cycles

Dayforce Cloud Extensions

Custom code bolted onto the SaaS platform. 3 of 18 gaps in development. Vendor-dependent, maintenance-heavy.

Partial coverage
"GC HR and Pay operations rely on technological capabilities to meet these requirements and would not be able to operate without addressing these gaps through either simplification of HR and pay business rules... or cloud extensions in Dayforce to meet the unique needs of the GC." — NextGen HR & Pay Final Findings Report, p. 38

No external solution currently exists to independently validate pay calculations
against authoritative collective agreement rules.

The Solution

Introducing ACCORD 🍁

A Canadian-made compliance validation layer that independently verifies every pay calculation against authoritative collective agreement rules. Built to complement Dayforce — not compete with it.

🔍 Validate

Independently verify pay run outputs against the authoritative rules encoded in collective agreements, TBS directives, and case law.

⚡ Flag

Identify discrepancies before they reach employees. Mass salary revisions, acting pay, rate-of-pay determinations — the hard problems.

📋 Audit

Provide a complete, traceable compliance trail. Every calculation tied to its authoritative source — article, clause, and provision.

ACCORD does not replace Dayforce. It provides the independent assurance layer that post-Phoenix governance demands.

Value Proposition

Why ACCORD Matters Now

🛡️ Post-Phoenix Assurance

Every department migrating to Dayforce needs confidence that pay will be accurate from day one. ACCORD provides independent, automated verification — the "second pair of eyes" that post-Phoenix governance demands.

🤝 Complements Dayforce

ACCORD does not replace or compete with Dayforce. It validates Dayforce's output against the same rules the GC already operates under. Both systems are stronger together.

⏱️ De-risks the Transition

As departments onboard in waves, ACCORD catches discrepancies during parallel runs and early production — when the cost of errors is highest and trust is most fragile.

📉 Reduces Backlog Risk

Phoenix still carries ~370,000 unresolved transactions. Preventing new errors during migration keeps the backlog from growing. ACCORD is the firewall.

🔎 Audit-Ready Compliance

Every validation produces a traceable audit trail — which collective agreement article, which TBS directive, which rule. Ready for OAG review from day one.
