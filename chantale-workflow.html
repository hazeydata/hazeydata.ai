The product in plain language

What ACCORD Actually Does

ACCORD is an API that answers one question:
"For this employee, in this situation, under this collective agreement, what are the rules?"

❓
Question Comes In
Dayforce or a comp advisor asks about an employee's entitlements
→
📖
ACCORD Looks It Up
Checks the CA rules, finds the right article, applies the logic
→
✅
Answer + Citation
Returns the rule with the exact article reference in EN + FR

Think of it like Google for collective agreements — but instead of links, it gives you the exact rule and the proof. That's it. That's the product.
